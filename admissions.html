Admission Requirements

As-salamu alaykum warahmatullahi wabarakatuhu,

We welcome prospective students and parents to Al-Meerod Academy. To ensure a smooth admission process, please find below the basic requirements for admission:

Required Documents

  1. Completed Application Form: Fill the online form on this website (or print and complete it).
  2. Medical Records:
    • Photocopy of Blood Group Test Result
    • Photocopy of Genotype Test Result
    • Photocopy of General Health Fitness Test Result
  3. Personal Documents:
    • Photocopy of Birth Certificate
    • Photocopy of National Identification Number (NIN) or International Passport
  4. Academic Records:
    • Last result obtained from the previous school (if applicable)
  5. Photographs:
    • 4 passport-sized photographs of the student
    • 2 passport-sized photographs each of both parents

Entrance Examination

A minimum score of 60% from the school entrance examination is required for admission (where applicable).

Note: You may upload soft copies of the documents when filling the online form (if available) or bring the hard copies along when you come to the school.

Please ensure that all required documents are submitted along with the completed application form. We look forward to welcoming your child to Al-Meerod Academy.

Jazakumullahu khayran.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is the school mixed (boys and girls)?

Yes. Al-Meerod Academy is a mixed school open to both boys and girls.

Do you offer boarding?

Yes. We offer both Day and Boarding options.

What curricula do you run?

We run a blend of Nigerian, Saudi and British curricula, plus Complete Complete Quran Memorisation and Arabic Madrasat.

What levels do you offer?

Creche, Nursery, Basic (Primary), Complete Quran Memorisation, Arabic Madrasat and Adult Education.

Is there an entrance examination?

Yes. A minimum score of 60% is required where an entrance examination is conducted.

How do I apply?

Fill the online Admission Form on this website, then bring the required documents to the school.
