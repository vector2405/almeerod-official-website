Academics

What We Offer

Al-Meerod Academy provides four major educational tracks designed to develop the complete Muslim individual.

📘

Western Education

Creche, Nursery and Basic (Primary) following Nigerian, Saudi and British curricula.

📖

Complete Quran Memorisation

Structured programme for complete memorisation of the Noble Quran with proper tajweed.

🕌

Arabic Madrasat

Idaadiyyah, Mutawassitah and Thanaawiyyah for deep Arabic and Islamic sciences.

👥

Adult Education

Special weekend classes for male and female adults (Saturday & Sunday).

1. Western Education

Modern academic foundation from the earliest years

Creche

Early Care & Stimulation

A warm, safe and stimulating environment for our youngest learners.

  • Age-appropriate play & learning
  • Loving care and supervision
  • Early social & motor skills
  • Introduction to Islamic values

Nursery

Foundation Years

Building curiosity, language and early literacy in a joyful setting.

  • Phonics & early reading
  • Numeracy foundations
  • Creative arts & play
  • Arabic alphabet & simple duas

Basic (Primary)

Basic 1 – 6

Solid academic base preparing pupils for secondary excellence.

  • Full Nigerian + British curriculum
  • Science, Mathematics, English & ICT
  • Islamic Studies & Arabic
  • Leadership and life skills

2. Complete Quran Memorisation

تحفيظ القرآن الكريم كاملاً

One of the distinctive strengths of Al-Meerod Academy is our structured Complete Quran Memorisation programme.

Qualified teachers guide pupils at a pace suitable for their age and ability, combining memorisation with understanding of basic meanings and proper tajweed.

We believe that the Quran is the foundation of a meaningful life. Our goal is to help every child build a strong and loving relationship with the Book of Allah from their earliest years.

  • ✓ Age-appropriate Hifz classes
  • ✓ Qualified and experienced teachers
  • ✓ Integration with daily school life
  • ✓ Regular revision and encouragement

3. Arabic Madrasat

المدرسة العربية

A structured Arabic and Islamic sciences pathway that builds strong language skills and deep religious knowledge.

Idaadiyyah

الإعدادية

Preparatory level focusing on foundational Arabic language, basic Islamic studies and Quranic reading.

  • Arabic reading & writing
  • Basic grammar (Nahw & Sarf)
  • Quran & Tajweed
  • Aqidah & Fiqh fundamentals

Mutawassitah

المتوسطة

Intermediate level that deepens Arabic proficiency and expands knowledge of Islamic sciences.

  • Intermediate Arabic language
  • Hadith & Seerah studies
  • Advanced Tajweed
  • Islamic history & manners

Thanaawiyyah

الثانوية

Secondary level preparing students for higher Islamic studies and strong scholarly foundation.

  • Advanced Arabic literature
  • Tafsir & Usul al-Fiqh
  • Hadith sciences
  • Leadership & Da’wah skills

4. Adult Education

تعليم تعليم الكبار

Al-Meerod Academy also offers special classes for adults (both male and female) who wish to learn or improve their Islamic knowledge, Arabic, and Quranic skills.

  • ✓ Open to both male and female adults
  • ✓ Classes hold only on Saturday and Sunday
  • ✓ Time: 8:00 am – 1:00 pm
  • ✓ Suitable for beginners and those seeking to strengthen their knowledge
