Our Facilities

A purpose-built environment designed for learning, worship and growth

Al-Meerod Academy is located in a peaceful setting in Ilobi Oke, Ajilete. Our campus features modern classroom blocks, a dedicated mosque, covered walkways and open spaces that support both academic excellence and spiritual development.

Modern Classroom Blocks

Well-designed classroom buildings with ample natural light, proper ventilation and comfortable outdoor seating areas for students and staff.

School Mosque

A beautiful mosque complete with minaret, providing a dedicated space for daily prayers, Quran memorisation sessions and Islamic programmes.

Covered Walkways

Protected corridors connecting the different blocks, ensuring pupils and staff can move comfortably between classes regardless of weather.

More of What We Offer

● Serene Environment

Located away from the noise of the city, our campus provides a calm atmosphere ideal for focused learning and reflection.

● Open Play Areas

Spacious outdoor grounds where children can play, exercise and develop social skills under supervision.

● Boarding Facilities

Comfortable accommodation for boarding students with proper supervision, meals and structured daily routines.

● Safe & Secure

A secure campus environment where the safety and wellbeing of every child is given top priority.

● Prayer & Worship

Regular congregational prayers and a strong culture of Islamic practice integrated into daily school life.

● Growing Campus

As a new school founded in 2026, we continue to develop and improve our facilities for the benefit of our students.
